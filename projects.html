Harvest Hope

Harvest Hope is an Irish potato famine historical game offering two distinct perspectives. As Mateo the farmer, you manage crops and quests, facing the mental burden of sustaining your community. As Albert the governor, you make macro-level political and financial choices, highlighting how government policies either save or break vulnerable communities. Developed using Figma, HTML and CSS.

Skills: Figma, Team Work, UI/UX Design, Prototyping, HTML, CSS
